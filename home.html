We’re building the next chapter with feedback from early testers. This is your place to find the testing steps, check development updates and tell us what could be better.

Join the tester group, opt in on Google Play, then install. Use the same Google account for all three steps.

Join the group before opting in. Sign in to Google Play with the same account you used to join the group, then opt in before installing.

  1. 01

    Join the Google Group

    Visit RoadSocial’s tester community with the Google account you use on your Android device. Group access may require approval.

    Visit tester group ↗
  2. 02

    Opt in to testing

    Sign in to the official Google Play testing page and accept the invitation to become a tester.

    Become a tester ↗
  3. 03

    Install & explore

    After opting in, install RoadSocial from Google Play. Explore the app and tell us what works—and what needs attention.

    Install RoadSocial ↗
Having trouble getting access?
