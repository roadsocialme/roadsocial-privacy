RoadSocial Account Deletion

RoadSocial users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your RoadSocial account, email:

roadsocialme@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your RoadSocial account and include the words "Delete My RoadSocial Account" in the subject line.

What will be deleted

Following verification of the request, we will delete the user's RoadSocial account and personal data associated with that account, except for information that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

Processing

Account deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period after the request has been verified.
